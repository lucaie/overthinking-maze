The memory maze

Each round shows three paths from the start square. Every stop on a path hides some points. You can click a stop to reveal its points, but each click costs you. The price is shown at the top of the round.

When you've seen enough, pick a path. Then the maze disappears and you walk your path from memory with the arrow keys. Walk it correctly and you get its points minus what you paid for clicks. Make a mistake and you only pay for the clicks.

2 practice rounds, then 16 real rounds (8 with cheap clicks and 8 with expensive ones, in random order). About 10 minutes. Your data appears at the end to copy.
