The memory maze

Each round shows three paths from the start square. Every stop hides some points, usually small, but one stop on each path hides a big amount (good or bad), and it can be anywhere along the path.

You can click a stop to reveal its points, but each click costs you. The price changes from round to round and is shown at the top. Some rounds also have a time limit for looking.

When you've seen enough, pick a path. Then the maze disappears and you walk your path from memory with the arrow keys (6 steps). Walk it correctly and you get its points minus your click costs. Make a mistake and you only pay for the clicks.

4 practice rounds, then 24 real rounds in two parts (with and without a time limit). About 15 minutes. Please stay on this page while playing. Your data appears at the end to copy.
